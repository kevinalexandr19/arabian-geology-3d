    Arabian Platform

    Generalized stratigraphic column

    Outcrop succession of central and northern Arabia, coloured by the map's Level 1 classes. Column width shows resistance to erosion: wide, resistant units hold up the cuesta escarpments (▲). Bar heights follow approximate outcrop thickness (minimum height for thin units).

    Generalized after Powers et al. (1966, USGS Professional Paper 560-D), Le Nindre et al. (1990) and Sharland et al. (2001). Thicknesses are approximate type-area outcrop values; subsurface thicknesses toward the Gulf are much greater. Ages are simplified to the period or epoch.

      About the project

      Arabia 3D

      A generalized geological map of the Arabian Peninsula draped over 3D terrain, built to show how rock type controls relief: the Arabian Shield, the cuestas of the platform, the harrats, the sand seas and the Oman mountains.
